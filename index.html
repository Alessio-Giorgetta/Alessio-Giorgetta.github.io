About

Hi! I'm Alessio, a PhD student in Economics at Stanford.

My research interests revolve around the state. How do public organizations complement, substitute for and interact with markets? How should they be organized? I have worked on how the discretionary space bureaucrats hold shapes discrimination and inequality, how monitoring costs change the organization of bureaucracies, and how state-mandated minimum wages affect workers' outside options. I like a hint of ML and structural analysis, and if time and effort allow, I'll be the annoying person asking what's welfare maximizing!

Like most of us, I've changed houses and affiliations countless times, but my heart stays put in the Italian Alps. When not researching (and doing everything that comes along with it) you can find me trekking or climbing, or one day surfing, if I ever get used to being in California!
