Region boundary geometry: italy_regions.js
============================================

The polygon geometry in italy_regions.js is derived from Italian
administrative boundaries:

  - Original data: ISTAT (Istituto Nazionale di Statistica),
    "Confini delle unita amministrative a fini statistici".
    Copyright ISTAT, released under the CC-BY licence.
  - Intermediate processing: the openpolis/geojson-italy project
    (https://github.com/openpolis/geojson-italy), which converts the
    ISTAT data to GeoJSON and releases it under CC-BY 4.0.
    Boundaries as of June 2023 (repository master branch),
    downloaded 2026-08-12.
  - Local processing for this site: equirectangular projection,
    Douglas-Peucker simplification (tolerance 1.2px at 560px height),
    outer rings only, islets under 4px^2 dropped, keyed by
    ISO 3166-2:IT code.
